1. Terms & Conditions

By creating an account and using Taskify, you agree to the terms below. Please read them before signing up.

1.1 Eligibility

Taskify is intended for current University of Mpumalanga students and lecturers. Registration requires a valid @ump.ac.za email address and a student or staff number. You're responsible for keeping your login details confidential and for anything that happens under your account.

1.2 How the marketplace works

Taskify is a platform that lets UMP students and staff post and respond to:

  • Tasks — small jobs one student offers to another (e.g. tutoring, moving help, design work).
  • Sales listings — items students want to sell to other students.
  • Equipment rentals — items students make available for other students to borrow or rent.
  • Events — campus or student-organised events shared with the community.

Taskify only provides the platform that connects people — it is not a party to any agreement, sale, rental, or task that users arrange with each other. Users are responsible for agreeing on their own terms (price, condition, timing, handover, etc.) and for exercising reasonable care and judgment, the same way you would with any peer-to-peer arrangement. Taskify does not guarantee the quality, safety, legality, or completion of anything posted or agreed between users, and is not liable for disputes between users.

1.3 Payments

Taskify currently includes a "Simulate Payment" feature that is a demo/mockup only — it does not connect to a real payment gateway, and no real money moves through the app. Any actual payment for a task, item, or rental happens directly between the users involved, outside of Taskify, using whatever method they agree on (cash, EFT, mobile payment app, etc.). Taskify does not process, hold, or guarantee any payment, and is not responsible for payment disputes between users. If a real payment gateway is added in the future, this section will be updated first.

1.4 Content & conduct

When you post a task, sale, rental, or event (including any photos), you're responsible for making sure it's accurate, honest, and doesn't violate these terms. Prohibited content includes anything illegal, hateful, violent, sexually explicit, harassing, or fraudulent, and anything that isn't yours to sell or rent out.

To help keep the platform safe, text and images submitted for tasks, sales, equipment, and events are automatically screened by an AI content-safety service before or shortly after publishing. Content that's clearly against the rules is blocked outright; borderline content may still publish but gets flagged for a human admin to review. This is an automated process and can occasionally get things wrong in either direction — you can report content you believe is inappropriate, and you can appeal a removal by contacting an admin.

Taskify's admins may remove content, suspend, or terminate accounts that violate these terms, at their discretion, with notice where reasonably possible.

To the fullest extent allowed by law, Taskify and its creator aren't liable for losses arising from your use of the platform or from arrangements you make with other users. 2. Privacy Policy

This section explains what personal information Taskify collects, why, and what you can do about it. Taskify aims to follow the principles of South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) — collecting only what's needed, using it only for the purposes described here, and keeping it reasonably secure — even though, as a student project, it has not gone through formal registration as an "operator" with the Information Regulator.

2.1 What we collect

When you register and use Taskify, we collect:

  • Account details — full name, UMP email address, WhatsApp/phone number, student or staff number, faculty/department, and (for students) academic year, or (for lecturers) title, years of experience, office location, and consultation mode, where provided.
  • Your password — stored as a one-way hash, never in plain text, and never visible to Taskify staff.
  • Profile photo — if you upload one, it's stored with our image host (Cloudinary).
  • Content you create — tasks, sales listings, equipment listings, events, reviews, and messages you send to other users or to admins.
  • Usage & device data — basic technical data needed to keep you logged in and the app working (session tokens, and, if you opt in, a push-notification subscription tied to your browser).

2.2 How we use it

  • To create and secure your account (including email verification and optional two-factor authentication).
  • To show your listings, profile, and activity to other UMP users, as intended for a marketplace app.
  • To send you notifications about your account and activity (in-app, email, and — only if you enable them — browser push notifications). You can turn push notifications off at any time from the app.
  • To automatically screen listing text and images for content that violates these terms, using an AI content-safety service, and to let admins review flagged or reported content.
  • To respond to support requests and enforce these terms.

We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't sell it.

2.3 Who your data is shared with

Taskify shares data only with the service providers that make the app work, and only as much as each one needs to do its job:

  • Aiven — hosts the app's database (account details, listings, messages).
  • Render — hosts the app itself (backend and frontend).
  • Cloudinary — stores uploaded images (profile photos, listing photos).
  • Microsoft Azure AI Content Safety — automatically scans listing text and images for policy violations.
  • Brevo — sends account emails (verification, password reset, OTP codes, notifications).

Other UMP users can see the information you choose to put on your public profile and listings, plus your name when you post a task, sale, rental, event, or review. We don't share your data with advertisers or unrelated third parties, and we only disclose it beyond the above if required by law.

2.4 Your rights & data retention

You can review and update most of your account details from your profile page at any time. You can ask an admin to correct, export, or delete your personal data by contacting us (below) — we'll action reasonable requests as soon as we can. If you delete your account, your account details are removed; listings, reviews, and messages tied to your account may be retained in an anonymised or minimal form where needed to keep the platform's history consistent for other users (e.g. a completed task's record).

2.5 Contact

Questions about these terms, your data, or a moderation decision? Reach out through the in-app messaging to an admin, or contact the Taskify team directly.
