About Taskify

Built for the UMP community

Taskify is a marketplace built specifically for University of Mpumalanga students — a single place to post and accept tasks, rent out or borrow equipment, buy and sell with people on your own campus, and keep up with student events.

Every account is verified with a real UMP student or staff email, and trust runs both ways: students build a reputation through reviews, and can be personally endorsed by lecturers along the way.
