About Taskify

Built for the UMP community

Taskify is a marketplace built specifically for University of Mpumalanga students — a single place to post tasks and apply for them, rent out or borrow equipment, buy and sell with people on your own campus, and find market days and swaps.

Every account is verified with a real UMP student or staff email, and trust runs both ways: students and staff build a reputation through reviews of every task, sale and rental.

Ready to join your campus marketplace?

It only takes your UMP email and a couple of minutes.
